Camp Guide & Controls

🕹️ Movement & Controls

  • Mouse Cursor: Move your mouse or cursor to guide your scout across camp (pointer lock supported, so the cursor really is your scout).
  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move around camp and swim in waters.
  • Mouse Click / Tap: Interact with objects, doors, levers, and gadgets.
  • Right Click / Keys 1 to 8: Express yourself with camp emotes (Wave, Ponder, Love, etc.).
  • M Key: Mute / Unmute ambient camp sounds and music.
  • Esc Key: Release cursor pointer lock or open pause menu.

🧭 Things to Discover

  • 17 Seashells: Hidden along shores, secret rooms, and caves. Collect them all to unlock the Beachcomber badge and shop for exclusive camp gear!
  • Interactive Gadgets: Bonfires, waterslides, grand pianos, bells, merry-go-round, drums, cauldrons, and secret trapdoors.
  • Outdoor Movie Screen: Check the outdoor theater screen for live premiering films!
  • 🌳 Village Chaupal: Follow the wayfinding signs east past the clubhouse to find a huge banyan tree — four village women sit beneath it and share their daily struggles around girls' education. Talk to them, explore the issues, share solutions, and see the impact they dream of.
